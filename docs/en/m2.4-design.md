# Outpost M2.4: a batch of fixes for known problems, and reordering by dragging

> 2026-11-28. The user said "fix all the problems, then start M3", and added mid-way "the position of tracks and products can be changed by dragging them". Everything below is my call.

## Fixed

1. **Sessions no longer crowd DSH's session list** (M1 defect 9): Outpost opens a dozen sessions a day (daily runs, checks, recommendations, topics…); they used to be archived after 7 days, now after 1. Today's and yesterday's are still there and open from the Runs page; older ones are in DSH's archive.
2. **Highlighted new text reads as one stretch** (M1 defect 7): a reworded sentence, compared character by character, used to light up in many small pieces; pieces at most 3 characters apart, or with only punctuation, spaces and citations between them, are now one.
3. **English singular and plural** (M1 defect 8): a count of 1 reads in the singular ("1 source", "1 run", "tried again 1 time"…), in 13 places; Chinese is unchanged.
4. **DSH session titles follow the interface language**: the panel tells the background its language (when it opens, when the language changes, and when the background first answers), and new sessions are named in it: 「前哨 · 小豆 日更 2026-11-28」 in Chinese, "Outpost · 小豆 · daily 2026-11-28" in English. Titles of sessions already open stay.
5. **The brief's spending is as it stands**: it used to be the figure when the brief was written, without a map or topic paid for afterwards.
6. **Retrying a weekly suggestion from the Runs page still asks for new products only**: a weekly run is marked, and its retry carries the same request.
7. **A plain install no longer shows a preset "failed to load" in DSH**: the research preset with the browser (前哨调研员) is now registered by Outpost when it starts, and only when the two browser packages are installed; without them, only the preset without a browser is there (which research used anyway). The browser is Chrome on a Mac, else whatever Playwright finds. To turn the browser fallback off, remove the two browser packages from the profile (dev/README.md step 6).

## Reordering by dragging

- Tracks in the middle column can be dragged up and down (by their header row); a track's products can be dragged within that track. A blue line shows where it will land.
- Keyboard: with the focus on a track's header or a product's row, Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ moves it up or down a place.
- The order is kept (database version 7: a position for tracks and for a track's products) and is also the order of the daily queue (the ones on top run first). New tracks and new products go to the end.
- **My call**: a product is dragged within its own track only, not into another (changing track stays with "Move to another track…" in the product page's ⋯, so a slip of the mouse cannot move it, and perhaps start filling in cards that cost money).

## Version

0.2.4, tag m2.4. Going back to 0.2.3: first `sqlite3 <data dir>/outpost.sqlite 'PRAGMA user_version=6'`, then install the 0.2.3 package (the older build does not read the two extra columns); before installing 0.2.4 again, set it back to 7.

## How it is checked

- Unit tests: archiving after a day; joined highlights; singular and plural; session titles by language (Chinese, English); the panel reporting its language; the brief's spending; retrying a weekly suggestion; the browser preset registered only when installed, unregistered on stop, research waiting for it; the preset definition differing from the one without a browser only by the browser group; storing the order; dragging and Alt+↑/↓.
