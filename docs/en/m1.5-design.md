# Outpost M1.5 design: the interface follows the Desktop's language; tracks and products can be deleted and edited

> 2026-11-28. The user asked for two things: "keep Outpost's interface language in sync with the language in the Desktop's settings", and "tracks and products can be deleted, and changed, for example renamed". The user chose how deleting and editing behave (below); the rest are my calls, marked as such.

## 1. The interface follows the Desktop's language

### Why

M1.1 decided "the interface is always Chinese": when the dictionaries were registered, both the Chinese and the English slot got the Chinese one. Now the user wants it to follow the Desktop's language. The English dictionary has stayed in `locales.ts` all along, unused.

The user's Desktop has no language of its own set and follows macOS (English), so after the update Outpost shows English; choosing Chinese in the Desktop's Settings → General → Language makes Outpost Chinese.

### What changed

1. **The dictionaries are registered as they are**: Chinese in the Chinese slot, English in the English one. When the Desktop switches language, Outpost follows at once, without reopening. The Desktop also offers Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Portuguese; Outpost has no dictionary for those and falls back to English, by the Desktop's own rule.
2. **The fixed sentences the background writes are translated too** (my call). Some text on screen is not written by the interface but by Outpost's background, into the database or an API answer: 「今日总预算已用完」 (today's total budget is used up), 「原文打不开」 (the source could not be opened), 「前哨已停止」 (Outpost was stopping), 「找不到这个产品」 (cannot find this product), 「超过 30 分钟没有结束，已停止」 (stopped after running longer than 30 minutes) and so on. In the English interface they used to show up in Chinese (defect 15 of M1).
   - How: a table in `src/core/` with every fixed sentence in Chinese and English; those with parameters (minutes, retries, a directory) are recognised by pattern.
   - The background still writes Chinese, so the database does not change and older rows are recognised too; the interface looks a text up before showing it, shows one it recognises in the current language, and shows any other text (a provider's own error, a note the AI wrote) as it is.
   - Both sides use the one table, and a test checks that every sentence the background writes from it is recognised.
3. **The six default dimensions** (定位 positioning, 目标人群 audience, …) are named in the current language; a dimension the user renamed or added shows as written. The database keeps the Chinese names, and the research prompts are not affected.
4. **List punctuation** follows the language: 「、」 in Chinese, 「, 」 in English.

### Not changed

- **What the AI writes is not translated** (the user's choice): dossier cards, findings, the brief's one-line summary, recommendation reasons, check notes stay in the language they were written in. The prompts have the AI write Chinese.
- **Session titles in the Desktop's session list** (「前哨 · 小豆 日更 2026-11-28」) stay Chinese: the background names a session when it creates it, and does not know which language the interface is in.
- Tool descriptions and prompts for the research agents stay Chinese.

## 2. Deleting tracks and products

### What the user chose

- **Deleting a product erases it**, after asking once more. Its dossier, findings and its items in past briefs are erased; spending records stay, so today's budget still counts what was spent. Added again later, it is profiled from scratch (which costs the profile again).
- **Deleting a track deletes the products only it has**: the question lists them, with the tip "to keep one, move it to another track first" (moving already exists). Products also in another track stay there and only leave this one.

### What deleting a product erases (my call)

- Erased: the product, every dimension's card with all its versions, its key facts, pending card edits, all its findings, and the sources only those findings and cards cite; its place in every track and its entries in recommendation records; its icon file.
- Past briefs: its findings, its "quiet" line and its "did not run" lines come out of every day's brief. A brief's one-line summary is written by the AI and may still mention it; it is not changed.
- Kept: the run records (spending). Where a run record named the product, it now shows the name it had, with 「（已删除）」 (deleted), so the bill can still be traced. The follow-up chats opened in the Desktop are the user's own and are not touched.
- **It cannot be deleted while it is being profiled, updated or checked**; Outpost says "it is running, delete it once that is over". Cutting a session short and then cleaning up what it half wrote easily leaves debris; this is enough until someone really needs "stop it now".

### What deleting a track erases (my call)

- Erased: the track, its recommendation records, the remembered "which candidates were seen"; the products only it has, as above; and the rows of products it recommended that were not picked and that no other track recommended (they cannot be seen anyway).
- It cannot be deleted while its recommendation or a confirmation of a product added by name is running, or while a product to be erased has work running; the message is as above.
- Run records stay.

### Where

- Product: at the bottom of the 「⋯」 menu at the top right of the product page, 「删除产品…」 (Delete product…), which opens the question in the menu: "Delete “小豆”? Its profile, findings and its items in past briefs are deleted and cannot be brought back. Spending records stay." [Cancel] [Delete]. After deleting, the panel goes to today's brief.
- Track: see the "Edit track" page below, which ends with "Delete track".

## 3. Editing tracks and products

### What the user chose to make editable

A track's name and focus, a track's dimensions, a product's name and company, a product's official site.

### Editing a track (my call)

- Each track's header in the middle column gets a small 「⋯」 (track settings) on the right; it opens the "Edit track" page: name, focus, dimensions, and "Delete track" at the bottom. A track still being picked can use this page too (the 「改」 on the picking page stays).
- Dimensions: the six defaults can be renamed and removed too, and new ones added. **Renaming keeps the cards** (the internal key does not change); a removed dimension's cards stay stored, out of sight (as with "take out of a track"); for a new dimension, the products already tracked **get their cards filled in at once**, which costs a little. That is how it already worked (adding a dimension while picking fills it in at once too); when I asked the user, I said "filled in at the next research run" — corrected here: it is at once, and the page says so. At least one dimension, at most 20, no name twice in one track.
- Deleting a track: a block at the bottom of the page says which products go with it (those only in this track), and it is done only after a second click, 「确认删除」 (Delete for good).

### Editing a product (my call)

- The product's 「⋯」 menu gets 「编辑名称和官网…」 (Edit name and site…), which opens a small form in the menu: name, company, official site. The site may be a web address or a domain, several separated by commas; only the domain is kept (lower case, without www.), in the same form recommendations keep it.
- After a rename, research and checks use the new name; earlier findings and the wording of cards do not change.
- A name and company that read the same as another product **being tracked** (like 「千问Go!」 and 「千问Go」): refused, with "Outpost already follows “X”. To merge them, delete one of the two". The same name as a product row that was only recommended and not picked: that row is merged in (its recommendation entries point at this product), with no error.

## API changes

- `POST products/:id/delete`, `POST tracks/:id/delete`: delete (POST, like the existing `unlink`, without relying on the DELETE method getting through the Desktop's web server). 409 while work is running.
- `PATCH products/:id` gains `name`, `company`, `officialDomains`.
- `PATCH tracks/:id` gains `dimensions` (the whole list; an entry with a `key` is an existing dimension, which may be renamed; one without is new), as an alternative to the existing `extraDimensions`.
- No tables are added and none change; deleting is a new kind of write (until now "rows are never deleted"; the note at the top of the migrations says so now).

## Version

0.1.5, matching m1.5.

## How it is checked

- Unit tests: the English dictionary is registered; the table recognises every fixed sentence; deleting a product erases it cleanly, takes it out of briefs, and keeps its runs with its name; refusal while work runs; which products go with a deleted track; rename clashes and merges; a renamed dimension keeps its cards.
- On the real Desktop: once installed, with the Desktop in English, Outpost should show English. The Desktop's language setting is not touched (changing it writes to the user's configuration); the Chinese interface is as before, which the tests cover. For deleting and editing, the real interface is only used to open the question and the forms and then cancel, so the user's data is neither deleted nor changed; the actual deleting and changing is covered by the API tests (on a scratch test database).

## Changed after review, and what is left (2026-11-28)

The independent review found nothing that blocks the release. Changed:

- Deleting a product now waits for all its work: in the tens of seconds between a profile session's end and the start of its check, it used to be deletable, and the check then failed with "Unknown product"; now deleting waits for the check. It also waits while another track's "add by name" confirmation has just handed this product in.
- Saving the picking page's 「改」 looks up each dimension's key by the track's own names first, so a same-named dimension that another track renamed no longer drags it to another key (which used to hide its cards and start a top-up).
- In English, removing "Audience" and typing it back by its English name brings back 目标人群 (audience) with its cards instead of making a new dimension; the page also checks for names given twice by the names it will store.
- After 取消 (Cancel) in the product menu's two new forms, the focus goes back to the entry that opened them (as the budget does), so the keyboard keeps working.
- A card's first history entry 「建档」 (profile built) and the corner quotes around a source's quote are in English in the English interface.
- A deleted product no longer comes back into a day's "quiet" or "did not run" lines when that brief is written again; an icon still downloading when its product is deleted is removed once it lands.
- The picking page allows as many custom dimensions as the defaults the track keeps leave room for (20 in all).

Left: a few English plurals such as "1 products" are not handled (the existing copy's convention); the track-delete question was reworded to avoid one.
