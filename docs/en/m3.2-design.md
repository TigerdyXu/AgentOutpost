# Outpost M3.2 design: searching in the panel

> 2026-11-28. After M3.1 the user chose "search, notes, a weekly recap, step by step, without stopping". This is the first step. How is my call, summed up under "My calls".

## What the user sees

- A search box under the middle column's title, reading "Search Outpost".
- Type the words; after a short pause (about 0.25 s) the results show on the right; Enter shows them at once; Esc empties the box. Opening another page empties it.
- The results page is titled "Search: “会员”", says "N matches", and groups the hits by kind, each group with its count:
  - **Products**: the name, then the company and tracks. Opens the product page.
  - **Tracks**: the name and the part of what it follows that matched. Opens the track page.
  - **Dossier cards**: product · dimension, and a snippet with the words in bold. Opens the product page with that card expanded and scrolled to.
  - **Findings**: product · date (with "Unconfirmed" or "Expired without proof" where they apply), and a snippet; when the words were found in "why it matters", the finding's headline shows too. Opens the product's Feed, scrolled to that finding and outlined; an expired one's fold is opened first.
  - **Topics**: the question and its track, and a snippet. Opens the topic page.
- At most 20 hits of a kind are listed; for more it says "N more not listed; try more specific words".
- With nothing typed it says how to search; with nothing found, "Nothing in Outpost matches “x”. Try other words, or fewer of them."
- Free: it searches only what Outpost has stored.

## How it matches

- Words separated by spaces; **every word must be there** (they may sit in different parts of the same thing, one in a card's summary and one in its text, say).
- Case and full/half width do not matter ("ＭＵＳＥ" finds "Muse"). No pinyin or fuzzy matching.
- What is searched is what the panel shows:
  - products being tracked, paused or profiled (not mere candidates);
  - tracks that are not archived (name and what they follow);
  - each dimension's current dossier card (summary, bullets, text; citation marks [n] aside), only in the dimensions the product's tracks list, as on the product page;
  - the findings the product's Feed shows: verified, unconfirmed, expired without proof (not unchecked or withdrawn ones);
  - topics' questions and their latest version's conclusion cards.
- Not searched: sources' titles and quotes, older card versions, the run log.

## My calls

1. The box sits under the middle column's title, always in sight; results fill the main area. No shortcut such as ⌘K or ⌘F: the Desktop uses those.
2. Search as you type (after a 0.25 s pause), at once on Enter; on a narrow panel Enter also closes the column.
3. Every word must be there; case and width aside; no pinyin or typo matching (the data is small, so precision wins).
4. The scope is what the panel shows, as above; findings newest first, everything else in the middle column's order.
5. At most 20 hits of a kind.
6. A dossier hit opens its card expanded; a finding hit opens the Feed scrolled to it and outlined.
7. Like other pages, the search page is where the panel reopens next time, with its words in the box.
8. Version 0.3.2, tag m3.2; no database change.

## API

- `GET search?q=`: `products`, `tracks`, `cards`, `findings`, `topics`, each hit with a `snippet` (a piece of the text and where the words are in it), and `more` (how many of each kind are not listed). A `q` that is blank once trimmed finds nothing; over 60 characters is a 400.
- The product route gains `finding` (the finding to scroll to; not remembered for next time); a new `search` page.

## How it is checked

- Unit tests: splitting the words, width and case; every word required; the snippet from the field holding the most words, long fields cut with ellipses, citation marks and line breaks dropped; products by name or company, tracks, cards (only listed dimensions), findings (only the statuses the panel shows, newest first), topics; candidates and archived tracks left out; 20 per kind and "N more"; a blank and an over-long query at the API.
- The panel: the results page's groups, counts, bold words, "N more", when a finding's headline shows, where each hit opens; nothing typed, nothing found, the English singular; the box searching after a pause, at once on Enter (closing the column), emptied by Esc and by another page; the Feed scrolled to the finding and outlined, an expired one's fold opened.
