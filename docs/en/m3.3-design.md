# Outpost M3.3 design: your own notes

> 2026-11-28. The user chose "search, notes, a weekly recap, step by step, without stopping"; this is the second step. How is my call, summed up under "My calls".

## What the user sees

- **The product page**: under the title, "+ Add a note: what you think of this product, what to watch". It opens a text box (at most 2000 characters, the count at its corner) with "Save" and "Cancel". Once saved it shows as a lightly tinted "My note" block, with the day it last changed and "Edit".
- **Every finding's card** (Today's brief, a product's Feed): "Add a note" beside "Ask more" (at most 500 characters). A note shows under the card, with its day and "Edit" too.
- While writing: ⌘↩ saves, Esc gives up; "Delete the note", or saving it empty, removes it. A failed save says why under the box, and the words stay.
- **Search** (M3.2) searches notes: a hit found there is tagged "My note", with the words.
- **Ask more**: the follow-up chat's first message carries your notes on the product and on the finding, marked "my view, not a source; do not cite it as a fact".
- **Other chats looking Outpost up** (`outpost_lookup`, M3.1): the product and its findings of 14 days carry `yourNote`, described as the user's own view, to take into account but never cite as a fact.
- Free: notes live in Outpost's own data only.

## My calls

1. **One note per product and per finding**, editable, not a thread of comments; it shows the day it last changed.
2. At most 2000 characters on a product, 500 on a finding.
3. A product's note sits under the product page's title (seen from every tab); a finding's note is on its card, with "Add a note" beside "Ask more".
4. **Research and checks never read notes**: a note is your view and should not steer what the AI looks for or how it judges. Search, Ask more and other chats looking Outpost up use them, always marked as your words.
5. "Delete the note" asks nothing more: the whole text is in the box before you delete it, and only that one note goes.
6. Deleting a product deletes its note and the notes on all its findings.
7. This release has notes on products and findings only; tracks and topics can get them later if needed.
8. Version 0.3.3, tag m3.3; the database goes to version 8 (a new `notes` table).

## API and data

- A new table `notes` (kind product or finding, the thing's id, the text, when it last changed), one per thing; database version 8.
- `POST notes/:kind/:id` (body `{ text }`) writes or removes (blank removes), answering `{ note }`; too long is a 400, no such product or finding a 404.
- `GET products/:id` gains `note`; `GET products/:id/findings` and `GET brief` gain `notes` (by finding id).
- Search hits on products and findings gain `inNote`.

Going back to 0.3.2: first `sqlite3 <data dir>/outpost.sqlite 'PRAGMA user_version=7'`, then install the 0.3.2 package (the older build neither reads nor writes the extra table); set it back to 8 before installing 0.3.3 again.

## How it is checked

- Unit tests: one note written, replaced, removed when blank; read in bulk by id; deleting a product removes its note and its findings' notes and no one else's; the API writes, reads back (product page, Feed, brief), removes, 400 when too long, 404 for no such thing, 400 for wrong arguments; search finds by notes and says so; `outpost_lookup` carries `yourNote`; Ask more's first message carries the notes, or "（没写）" (none).
- The panel: writing a product note (focused at once, nothing to save when empty, the count, shown once saved, the text brought back to edit, back to "+ Add a note" once deleted); a failed save says why and keeps the words; on a finding card Esc gives up, opening again starts from what is kept, ⌘↩ saves, and "Add a note" goes once there is one; notes the host sent are shown; search hits tagged "My note".
