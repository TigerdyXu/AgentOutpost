# Outpost M2.3 design: topics (one question looked into across products)

> 2026-11-28. The third M2 release (design §5.5 right half, §5.6). On 2026-11-25 the user settled that the track page has topics and what a topic looks like (mockup `docs/mockups/5-赛道页.html`, "how topics work" at the bottom); the trade-offs made while building it are my calls, summed up under "My calls".

## What the user sees

- **The track page** gets a 「专题」 (Topics) block, between the map and the comparison table: the track's topics, each row its question, how many products, the date of the first version, 「一次性」 (Once) or 「每周更新」 (Weekly), and what the last version changed; one being researched reads 「正在深挖…」 (Looking into it…). At the bottom, "+ Ask a question for the AI to look into".
- **A new topic**: a small form on the track page: the question (≤60 characters), which products (by default every product of the track that is being followed), Once or Weekly, the cost ("About ¥1–2 each time: one research session and one check"), and "Start".
- **The topic page**: the question, its track, its products (each opening the product page), the first version's date, how often; the conclusion cards first (title, one-line conclusion, bullets), "Expand" for the full text and its sources, whose [n] open the quotes as on a dossier card; for a weekly one, "this version: …" and the dates of the versions. At the top right, "Update now" (another round, at another round's cost), and buttons to switch Once/Weekly; deleting the topic at the bottom.
- **The product page** gets a 「专题」 (Topics) tab listing the topics about that product.

## How it is made

- **One research session** (named "Topic" on the Runs page): given the question, the products (name, company, official site) and their dossiers' conclusions, it searches the web and hands in 2–6 conclusion cards: a title (≤20 characters), a one-line conclusion (≤40), 1–3 bullets, the full text (≤2000, each sentence marked [n] with its source), the sources (each with a quote copied word for word from the page), and which products the card is about; its own inferences are marked. On an update it also gets the previous version, changes only what moved, and says in one sentence what this version changed.
- **One check session** (named "Topic check"): the same checker and the same rules as dossier cards: it opens every source to find the quote; a source opened without the quote is void, sentences resting only on void sources are cut, and a card with no valid source left is dropped.
- Only when at least one card is left after the check does it become a new version; with none left, there is no new version this time, and the topic page says why.
- **Weekly topics**: once the newest version is 7 days old, another round runs after each day's brief, maps and weekly suggestions; a skipped or failed one is tried the next day, at most once a day (as with weekly suggestions).
- Cost: both sessions count in that day's budget and queue like daily updates (how many run at once is limited by "products researched at the same time").

## Deleting and renaming

- Deleting a track deletes its topics, with the sources only they cite.
- Deleting a product takes it out of every topic; a topic left with no product is deleted. Cards already written keep their text (as dossiers do).
- Renaming a product: topics show the new name (they keep product ids).

## My calls

1. Topics sit between the map and the table on the track page, not to the right of the map as in the mockup (the map needs the width).
2. A topic has 1–8 products, by default every product of the track being followed; the question ≤60 characters.
3. The check uses the dossier cards' checker and rules (no new set); "official" is the checker's call, as in M1.6.
4. A weekly topic counts 7 days from its newest version; "Update now" by hand moves the next round back too. A round with no version is tried again the next day, and after 3 in a row the topic stops trying by itself (added after review).
5. On the Runs page, topic runs and topic checks cannot be retried; to run again, use "Update now" on the topic page (a retry would not know which version it builds on).
6. Version 0.2.3, tag m2.3.

## Changed after review (2026-11-28)

The independent review found nothing that blocks the release. Changed:

- **A topic check runs on the check model**: it used to run on the research model, so the model chosen for checks in the settings was ignored, and the cost was priced on the wrong model.
- **A product a topic round is about cannot be deleted while the round runs**: before, it could, and the round went on running and spending, then failed. Now, as with daily runs, it says "running; delete it once that is over".
- **Sources the check threw out are deleted at once**: before, they stayed in the database, and deleting the topic did not remove them either.
- **After 3 rounds in a row with no version, the topic stops trying by itself**: each round is ¥1–2, and a question without checkable sources would be retried, and paid for, every day. Now it stops after the third, and the topic page says "Three rounds in a row made no version, so Outpost no longer tries by itself"; after adjusting the question or the products, "Update now" still runs a round. (My call, added here: the "tried the next day" above is at most 3 tries.)
- A half-done round left by an Outpost that did not stop cleanly (killed, say) is marked "Outpost was stopping" on the next start, and the topic page shows why.
- "Update now" is refused for an archived track; a topic page left open after the topic was deleted elsewhere says "Cannot find this topic"; the previous version's date given to the researcher is the local date.
- A one-off topic with no version yet that will be tried again also shows "next …" on its page.

## API and data

- New tables `topics` (question, track, products, how often) and `topic_versions` (each round: a draft while researched, then after the check a final version, or "no version" with the reason), database version 6. Sources stay in the `sources` table; deleting a topic deletes the sources only it cites.
- Two new run kinds: `topic` (Topic) and `topicCheck` (Topic check), named by the topic's question on the Runs page.
- `GET tracks/:id/overview` gains `topics`; `POST tracks/:id/topics` starts one; `GET topics/:id`; `POST topics/:id/update` (Update now); `PATCH topics/:id` (how often); `POST topics/:id/delete`; `GET products/:id/topics` (the product page's tab). While a round runs, updating, deleting the topic and deleting its track are 409s.
- In topic mode the checker's tools see only this round's cards; its prompt is the dossier check's, with "the product" replaced by "the topic's products" and "the company" by their companies.

Going back to 0.2.2: first `sqlite3 <data dir>/outpost.sqlite 'PRAGMA user_version=5'`, then install the 0.2.2 package; the older build neither reads nor writes the two extra tables. Before installing 0.2.3 again, set it back to 6.

## Cost

- About ¥1–2 a round: one research session (web search, opening pages) and one check session. A weekly topic has one round a week.

## How it is checked

- Unit tests: a whole round (cards handed in, checked, those that hold kept, written as version 1, the researcher's summary as "what this version changed"); the second version is given the first; no version, with the reason, when nothing held, no card was handed in, or a session failed; the card refusals (another product, a citation out of range, more than 6); when the next round is due; what deleting a product or a track does to topics and their sources; the routes' 409s and 400s; the track page's topic list and form, the topic page, the product page's tab (Chinese and English).
